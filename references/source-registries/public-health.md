# Public Health and Social Medicine Source Registry

Use for public health, hygiene, epidemiology, medical statistics, medical law, medical ethics, medical safety, healthcare systems, and health informatics.

## Recommended ExamSubject

- `公衆衛生学`
- `医療情報学`
- `医療倫理学`
- `医療安全学`

## Sources to Prefer

- 厚生労働省: population statistics, health policy, infectious disease law, healthcare systems
- e-Gov法令検索: laws, ordinances, and enforcement rules
- National Institute of Infectious Diseases: surveillance and infectious disease facts
- Cabinet Office and related government sources for demographics and social statistics
- WHO/OECD when international comparison is central
- Official national survey pages for current statistics

## Notes

Always verify the year for statistics, laws, notification categories, vaccination programs, and screening programs. State exact years when a question depends on temporal data.
