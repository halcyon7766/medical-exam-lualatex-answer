# Surgery Source Registry

Use for surgery, orthopedic surgery, anesthesiology, emergency medicine, intensive care, rehabilitation, and procedure-heavy exams.

## Recommended ExamSubject

- `外科学`
- `整形外科学`
- `麻酔科学`
- `救急医学`
- `リハビリテーション医学`

## Sources to Prefer

- Japanese surgical society and organ-specific surgical guidelines
- Japanese Orthopaedic Association and related guidelines for orthopedics
- Japanese Society of Anesthesiologists guidance for anesthesia, airway, perioperative safety
- Japan Resuscitation Council guidelines for resuscitation
- Japanese Association for Acute Medicine guidance for emergency and critical care
- Trauma, burn, poisoning, and disaster medicine guidance from relevant Japanese societies

## Notes

For surgical questions, emphasize indication, contraindication, initial stabilization, operative method, complications, and anatomy. For emergency questions, prioritize time-critical first actions.
