# Internal Medicine Source Registry

Use for internal medicine and organ-system clinical exams.

## Recommended ExamSubject

- `内科学`
- `循環器病学`
- `呼吸器病学`
- `消化器病学`
- `腎臓病学`
- `内分泌・代謝病学`
- `血液学`
- `腫瘍学`
- `感染症学`
- `膠原病学`
- `神経内科学`

## Sources to Prefer

- Japanese Society of Internal Medicine and organ-specific Japanese society guidelines
- Japanese Circulation Society guidelines for cardiovascular disease
- Japanese Respiratory Society guidelines for respiratory disease
- Japanese Society of Gastroenterology and related societies for GI/hepatobiliary disease
- Japanese Society of Nephrology guidelines for kidney disease
- Japan Diabetes Society and endocrine society guidance for diabetes/endocrine disease
- Japanese Society of Hematology and oncology guidelines for hematology/oncology
- National Institute of Infectious Diseases and MHLW for infectious disease rules
- Japanese College of Rheumatology and related guidance for rheumatology

## Notes

Prefer guideline-based first-line diagnosis and treatment. If an older exam expects an older recommendation, separate `出題時点の扱い` from `現在の推奨`.
