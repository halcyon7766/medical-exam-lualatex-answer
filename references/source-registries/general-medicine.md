# General Medicine Source Registry

Use for mixed or uncertain medical exams.

## Recommended ExamSubject

- `医学一般`

## Sources to Prefer

- 厚生労働省: laws, public programs, infectious disease notifications, national statistics
- こども家庭庁: child and maternal health policies
- PMDA: drug labels, safety information
- e-Gov法令検索: Japanese laws and ministerial ordinances
- ICD/WHO material: international disease classification
- Major Japanese academic society guidelines by topic

## Notes

When the subject is mixed, organize the booklet by broad sections such as `基礎医学`, `臨床医学`, and `社会医学` while preserving question order.
