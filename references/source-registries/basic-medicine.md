# Basic Medicine Source Registry

Use for anatomy, histology, embryology, physiology, biochemistry, pharmacology, pathology, microbiology, immunology, parasitology, and forensic medicine.

- `解剖学`
- `組織細胞生物学`
- `生理学`
- `生化学`
- `薬理学`
- `病理学`
- `微生物学`
- `免疫学`
- `寄生虫学`
- `法医学`

## Sources to Prefer

- Japanese academic society guidelines when the question touches diagnostic classification or disease entities
- PMDA drug labels and safety communications for pharmacology
- National Institute of Infectious Diseases for microbiology and immunology topics
- WHO/CDC pages for globally standardized infectious disease facts when Japanese sources are insufficient
- Standard classification references such as WHO tumor classifications when pathology classification is central

## Notes

For stable mechanisms, textbook-level explanation is acceptable, but diagnostic categories, pathogens, drugs, and classifications should be checked against current authoritative sources.
