# Clinical Specialties Source Registry

Use for pediatrics, obstetrics and gynecology, psychiatry, dermatology, ophthalmology, otolaryngology, urology, and radiology.

## Recommended ExamSubject

- `小児科学`
- `産婦人科学`
- `精神医学`
- `皮膚科学`
- `眼科学`
- `耳鼻咽喉科学`
- `泌尿器科学`
- `放射線医学`

## Sources to Prefer

- Pediatric: Japanese Pediatric Society, MHLW vaccination pages, JRC neonatal resuscitation, disease-specific Japanese societies
- Obstetrics/Gynecology: Japan Society of Obstetrics and Gynecology guidelines and perinatal guidance
- Psychiatry: DSM/ICD material, Japanese Society of Psychiatry and Neurology, MHLW mental health resources
- Dermatology: Japanese Dermatological Association guidelines
- Ophthalmology: Japanese Ophthalmological Society and disease-specific guidelines
- Otolaryngology: Japanese otolaryngology/head and neck surgery societies
- Urology: Japanese Urological Association guidelines
- Radiology: Japan Radiological Society and modality-specific guidance

## Notes

For specialty exams, identify whether the question is asking a general national-board style fact or a specialty-specific guideline detail. Use the specialty source for treatment and diagnostic criteria whenever available.
